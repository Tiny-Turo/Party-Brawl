# Curse

**IDEA:** Every 20 seconds or more a curse befalls upon the players and they must continue their task.

**EXAMPLES:**

- **Blindness** - Each player has a circle in which they can only see that far, this way they have to remember where the items are and have difficulty finding them.
- **Rats** - Rats come and try to grab the items you have laying about
- **Cacti** - Cacti fall from the sky and break from the ground and if you hit them you lose what you have on you
